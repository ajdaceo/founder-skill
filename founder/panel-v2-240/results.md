## What would flip a no

- Delivery to my door, plus a few real reviews from people who bought from them.
- Delivery or collection close to me, and a price nearer £160-180 for my exact fitment.
- Delivery or fitting at my address for a small fee, or a few good reviews from real buyers.
- A set in my exact fitment for about £180 that I can check in person with a clear balancer report, or a few real reviews from local buyers.
- A set that fits my car at around £160-180, plus a few reviews or photos of past sets I could judge for myself.
- Seeing the set in person first with the bend and crack check done in front of me, in my exact PCD and offset, or a few real reviews from local buyers.
- A handful of real reviews from people who bought and fitted a set, or a seller history I can check, plus a mate or a wheel shop confirming the set is straight when I view it.
- A set with decent tyres included at a clear price, plus a few real reviews or photos of other customers' finished sets.
- A set that comes with tyres that have good stated tread depth (say 5mm+) for around the same money, for a fitment that matches my car.
- A price nearer £160-180 for a set that I can check myself in person, ideally with a bend and crack check done in front of me.
- A set listed for my exact car at about £180-200, or a seller willing to haggle down to that after I've seen the set in person.
- A few real reviews or a seller history I can check, or a price around £180 with tyres included so I'm not gambling on an unknown.

Buyers say they would buy **1.0 times** in the first month on average.
