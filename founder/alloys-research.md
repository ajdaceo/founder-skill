## Bolt patterns (PCD)
- Confirmed in search: VW Golf, Audi A3, Mercedes C-Class share 5x112 (centre bore differs: check it) ([RaRev](https://rerev.com/bolt-patterns/5x112/)).
- Search conflicted or was silent: Ford Fiesta/Focus (4x108 on older, 5x108 on later), BMW 3 Series, Vauxhall Corsa/Astra.
- From my own memory, NOT verified here: BMW 3 Series 5x120; Focus 5x108; Corsa 4x100; Astra 5x105 (older) or 5x115; Polo 5x100. Check each against a fitment database before buying any set.

## What it suggests (my reading)
1. Best volume: 5x112 sets (Golf, A3, plus other VW Group and Mercedes), then Ford 5x108 (Focus/Fiesta), in 16-18 inch. One bolt pattern covers many cars, so more buyers per set.
2. Small-car wheels (Corsa, Polo, older Fiesta, 15-16 inch) are everywhere but likely sell cheaper.
3. Cheap buy, small profit means every set has a cost ceiling; the sold-listing check decides what you can pay.
