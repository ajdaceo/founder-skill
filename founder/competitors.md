## Stage 1: alloy wheels

- Refurb shops: about £55 a wheel in a forum post, £220 offered to respray four, as low as £40 a wheel ([MoneyExpert guide](https://www.moneyexpert.com/car-insurance/guides/how-much-does-it-cost-to-refurbish-alloy-wheels) plus forum posts; undated).
- A used 17in set with tyres, needing refurb, listed at £200 on [eBay](https://www.ebay.de/itm/256924133889) (sold).
- New sets: £400-£1,000+ (one guide).
- I found no local Kent seller of used or refurbished sets.
- **My inference, not a finding:** if refurb costs ~£220 a set outsourced, a £200 used set plus refurb costs ~£420 before tyres, fuel and time, so a £80-£200 profit only works if you do the refurb yourself and buy well below £200. Needs your own numbers (CFO).

## Positioning (text map)

- **Stage 2, axes: price vs. trust.** Bottom-left: Rochester budget dealers (cheap, trust unknown). Middle: Big Motoring World (volume, big warranty, mixed reviews). Upper: Arbat-style premium dealers with 3-month warranty. Private sellers sit at cheapest price, lowest trust. The empty spot is "£2k-£5k with proof": inspection report shown, written aftercare, local face.
- **Stage 3, axes: price vs. car age.** Cheap and classic: £150-£250 Silver Spirits/Bentley Turbo R (Bluemoon, Lady R). Modern and mid-price: Mercedes E/S at £200-£230 (Platinum, All Night Taxis). Expensive: Phantoms and London operators £500+. Nobody found offering modern prestige (S-Class, Range Rover, 7 Series) in Medway/Bexley at a clear price, but my search was thin so this could be a search gap.

## The gap

- **Stage 2: plausible but unproven.** Everyone advertises a 3-month warranty, so a warranty alone is not a gap. The only complaint I found is the big dealer's warranty not paying out, which suggests a simple, plainly-written, small-scale promise could stand out. But I only have one dealer's complaints. Verify with real local reviews before building on it.
- **Stage 3: no gap shown.** Wedding cars start at £150-£250 and the closest incumbent is excellently reviewed. Competing there on price is a loser; at those prices a modern prestige car plus insurance may not cover costs (CFO to test). The only possible opening is modern prestige for proms and events, where I found no local data. That must be tested with real quotes and the consumer panel, not assumed.
- **Stage 1: no gap evidence found.**

## The threat

A Rochester or Dartford dealer adding an inspection report costs them an afternoon. For stage 3, Lady R and similar firms can add a modern car at will, and Platinum and All Night Taxis already run Mercedes.
