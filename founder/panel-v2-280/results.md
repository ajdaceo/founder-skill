A new seller with no reviews doesn't make me rush either." (P003) · "£280 is over a week and a half of my take-home, and I can usually find a decent used set on Marketplace for £150-200 if I'm patient and haggle. ## What would flip a no

- Delivery or fitting at my door, plus a handful of genuine reviews from other buyers.
- Delivery to my door, or a price nearer £200 for a set that fits my car.
- Delivery or fitting at my door or a nearby garage, and a few reviews or photos of finished sets on a car like mine.
- Dropping to about £200 a set, or letting me see them on a balancer in person and haggle on the day.
- A set of my exact fitment at about £200 that I can inspect on collection, or a few real reviews from local buyers.
- A price around £200 for a set in my fitment, or seeing the set in person first with the bare wheel and balancer report as stated, which would let me judge it myself.
- A set that matches my car's exact PCD and offset, around £220, that I can see in person with the balancer report and tread depth shown. A few real buyers vouching for the seller would also help.
- A few real reviews from local buyers, or a mate who's bought from them, plus being able to get the wheels checked on a balancer or by a tyre shop before handing over money.
- Tyres with decent stated tread included at the same or a small bit more price, or a few real reviews from local buyers.
- Tyres included with decent stated tread depth (say 6mm+) at the same £280, or a clear price cut to around £200 so I can afford tyres separately.
- Around £200 a set for my exact fitment, with the chance to see it spun on a balancer at collection and haggle in person.
- Around £200-220 a set for my exact fitment, with tyres thrown in or a few reviews from locals.
