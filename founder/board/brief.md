# Good Car Club (GCC)

- What it is: A one-person vehicle business in three stages, each funded by the one before: (1) flip used alloy wheels, (2) flip undervalued used cars, (3) prestige car hire (chauffeur-driven first, self-drive later).
- Who it is for: Stage 1 - local buyers of used alloys (private/online). Stage 2 - local drivers buying a £2,000-£5,000 car: first-car buyers and parents (35%), commuters replacing a car (40%), families needing space (25%). Stage 3 - people hiring prestige cars for weddings, proms, events and weekends.
- What it sells, at what price: Stage 1 - refurbished alloy sets, target profit £80-£200 a set. Stage 2 - used cars bought at £2,000-£5,000, prepped and sold with a history check, fresh MOT and inspection report, target profit £500-£1,000 a car. Stage 3 - chauffeur-driven prestige car hire (prices not yet set).
- Where and how: Based between Bexleyheath and Gillingham, north Kent (A2/M2 corridor, covering Medway, Dartford and Bexley). Sourcing/selling online and locally. If successful, relocate north of London to reach London and Home Counties markets.
- Budget and constraints: One person. Works at Tesco (about £14.70/hour) and will build GCC alongside the job until it consistently makes £3,000-£4,000 a month profit. Starting capital: NOT STATED (open question). Currency GBP, UK market.
