# Ops plan: GCC Stage 1, selling 8-10 refurbished alloy sets a month (GBP)

Built from the founder-ops steps. Anything marked ASSUMPTION is mine and not researched; replace it with numbers from your first sets. Sources are linked where I found them. Not legal, tax or insurance advice.

## 1. What 8-10 sets a month really means
- 8-10 sold means buying about 10-12 (ASSUMPTION: about 1 in 6 turns out cracked, bent or unsellable).
- That is 2-3 sets a week through the whole chain: find, collect, inspect, refurbish, photograph, list, answer, view, hand over.
- You have 3 days off a week, about 13 working days a month. 10 sets is roughly one set per working day, so you must batch (3-4 sets in the same prep stage at once).

## 2. The hours problem (the biggest finding)
| Refurb level | Hours per set (ASSUMPTION) | Hours/month at 8 | at 10 | per week at 10 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Clean, polish, touch-up only | 2 + 3 admin | 40 | 50 | 11.5 |
| Proper DIY refinish, batched | 6 + 3 admin | 72 | 90 | 20.8 |
| Proper DIY refinish, one by one | 16 + 3 admin | 152 | 190 | 43.9 |

"Admin" = sourcing, collection, photos, listing, messages, viewing (3 h is a guess). The 16 h comes from one guide saying a wheel can take up to four hours ([Who Can Fix My Car](https://www.whocanfixmycar.com/advice/alloy-wheel-repair-replacement)); the sources give no reliable cure times, so check product labels ([Holts](https://www.holtsauto.com/simoniz/news/can-i-refurbish-my-own-alloys/)).
**At about 20 hours a week (batched full refinish) the plan fits your 3 days off. At 44 hours it does not.**

## 3. Money per month (sale £260, 20% of purchases wasted)
| Sets sold | Buy price per set | Supplies per set | Monthly profit |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 8 | £60 | £50 | £1,104 |
| 8 | £90 | £80 | £576 |
| 8 | £120 | £80 | £288 |
| 10 | £60 | £50 | £1,380 |
| 10 | £90 | £80 | £720 |
| 10 | £120 | £80 | £360 |
Before fees, fuel, returns and tax. **To make £1,000 a month you need to buy whole sets at about £60-£90.** At £120 a set (and no free stock) you make £300-£650 for 40-90 hours of work.

**Hourly rate check** (profit per set / hours per set incl. admin): £60 over 9 h = £6.67 an hour; £100 over 9 h = £11.11; £150 over 9 h = £16.67; £150 over 5 h = £30. Tesco is £14.70 an hour before tax. Clean-and-polish sets bought well are where this beats Tesco.

## 4. Daily/weekly cycle (3 days off)
- **Day 1 (sourcing + intake):** collect sets bought during the week, inspect for bends/cracks, photograph "before" (bare wheel), strip/degrease, sand. Buy decisions follow the max-price rule below.
- **Day 2 (refurb batch):** repair kerb damage, prime, colour, lacquer 3-4 sets together. Let them cure as the product label says.
- **Day 3 (sell):** "after" photos, balancer/straightness check evidence, list, viewings, handovers.
- **Tesco days:** reply to messages, scan listings, line up collections. Viewings after work or on a day off.

## 5. Buying rule (the part that decides profit)
Max you pay for a whole set = sale price - supplies - profit you want - a wasted-purchase allowance.
At £260 sale, £80 supplies, £100 profit target: pay about £60-£80. At £50 supplies: £110.
Salvage singles (£35-£63 each) fail this rule; see `founder/cfo-wheels.md`.
Where to look (all asking prices, undated, check yourself): Gumtree and eBay sets ("got my set of 4 for £200 including shipping", "just over £200" with tyres, forum posts); breakers selling "from 100 for a set of four" ([Autobreakers, undated](https://www.autobreakers.co.uk)); [BreakerLink](https://breakerlink.com/parts/wheel). Phoning breakers and asking for job lots is cheap to test.

## 6. Kit (prices not researched unless shown)
Wheel stand, degreaser, filler, sandpaper 120-800 grit, etch primer (U-POL Acid #8 about £9.99 forum), primer, colour, lacquer, masks, PPE, balancer/straightness check or a tyre shop to check. A 5-can kit listing shows £35.79 (eBay, undated). Tyre machine: do not buy; sell wheels without tyres. If you sell with part-worn tyres, they need at least 2mm tread across the whole tyre and a permanent "PART-WORN" mark (from the desktop assistant's [SE Tyres](https://www.setyres.com/news-article/part-worn-tyre-warning) source; verify).

## 7. Channels and fees
- **Facebook Marketplace / Gumtree:** local, collection, no fee data found. Best fit for viewing-first sales.
- **eBay UK as a business seller:** Vehicle Parts & Accessories 9.5% up to £750 plus £0.40 per order over £10 and a 0.35% regulatory fee, with VAT on the fees ([eBay notice, Feb 2026](https://www.ebay.co.uk/sellercentre/news/2026-january/rate-card-change)). On a £260 sale that is about £26 before VAT, about £31 with VAT on the fees. That is roughly 12% of price and comes straight out of your £60-£100 profit.
- Track every set in one spreadsheet: bought for, supplies, hours, listed date, sold date, channel, buyer, return. Days-to-sell is not published anywhere, so this sheet is your only source.

## 8. Rules and paperwork (confirm each with the authority)
- **HMRC:** if trading income goes over £1,000 in a tax year you must register for Self Assessment ([ByteStart summary](https://www.bytestart.co.uk/self-employed-tax/trading-allowance/); confirm on gov.uk). The £1,000 allowance is on income before costs, so selling 4 sets can exceed it. Keep receipts. Check with an accountant before the first sale. Plan changes to the threshold were reported for 2027/28.
- **Consumer law:** selling regularly makes you a trader. Second-hand goods sold by a trader must be of satisfactory quality, fit for purpose and as described, with a 30-day short-term right to reject ([MoneyHelper](https://www.moneyhelper.org.uk/en/getting-help-and-advice/consumer-rights/consumer-rights-what-you-need-to-know)). **This is stronger than the 7-day return in your pitch and you cannot contract out of it.** Your 7-day undamaged return is an extra, for "changed my mind" cases; the 30 days applies to faults and misdescription. Confirm with Trading Standards or [Business Companion](https://www.businesscompanion.info). Private-seller rules do not protect you once you trade.
- **Insurance:** check home insurance for business use and viewings at your address, and whether public liability is worth it. Not researched.
- **Fitting:** buyers should tell their insurer about non-original wheels (forum advice); say so in your listing.

## 9. Risks
| Risk | Likely | Impact | Plan |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Can't find cheap whole sets | High | Kills margin | Test breakers/Gumtree buying in week 1 before spending on kit |
| Refurb eats hours | High | Under £10/hour | Sell clean-and-polish sets; buy only light-damage sets; time every set |
| Hidden crack/bend | Medium | Return, reputation | Check on balancer/at a tyre shop before listing; show the proof |
| Finish peels, buyer returns | Medium | Refund + bad review | Proper prep, follow label cure times, photo log |
| Wheel won't fit | Medium | Return | State PCD, offset, centre bore in every listing; don't list if unknown |
| Slow sales | Medium | Cash tied up | Max 4-5 sets in stock; drop to the £240 floor after 14 days |
| eBay fees eat margin | High if used | -£26 to -£31 a set | Prefer local sales; use eBay only for sets that need reach |
| Time (Tesco rota clash) | Medium | Missed viewings | Fixed viewing slots, message templates |
| Tax surprise | Medium | Fine/back-tax | Register and keep records from set 1 |
| No-shows at viewings | High | Wasted hours | Confirm the day before; cash/transfer on collection |

## 10. A 4-week pilot before scaling to 8-10
Weeks 1-4: 3-4 sets (start with your free set). Measure for each: buy price, supplies, hours, days to sell, channel. Pass if ALL hold:
- average buy price at or below £90 a set (or free), and
- total hours per set at or below 8, and
- sold within 14 days of listing, and
- profit per hour at or above £15 (so it beats Tesco's £14.70 before tax), and
- no unresolved returns.
If any fail, fix that one thing (price, hours, supply) and re-run. Do not scale volume until the pilot passes. Then re-run `/founder-cfo` on your real figures.
