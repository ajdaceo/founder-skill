Continuous execution loop

During every active execution session:

1. Recover the persistent objective, current milestone and business state.
2. Identify the most valuable action available.
3. Execute that action.
4. Verify its actual result.
5. Update the persistent state.
6. Identify the next most valuable action.
7. Execute it immediately.
8. Repeat.

Do not terminate a session merely because one task has been completed.

Do not stop after producing a research document or status report when further useful work remains available.

Complete as much meaningful work as the supported execution environment, usage allowance and authorised permissions reasonably permit.

Parallelise independent tasks where supported and useful.

Do not become trapped in indefinite marketplace comparisons.

When an opportunity passes the commercial gates, proceed towards validation rather than continuing to search for a theoretically perfect opportunity.

Research, infrastructure development and commercial execution may proceed in parallel where appropriate.

Valid stopping conditions

An execution session may stop when:

* Supported execution or usage limits are approaching.
* No useful authorised work remains.
* A critical owner approval blocks all meaningful progress.
* A material legal, financial, security or customer risk requires suspension.
* The programme stop-loss or review deadline has been reached.
* Further work would be commercially wasteful.

Completing an individual task is not a valid stopping condition by itself.

If one task is blocked, pursue another useful task.

Before stopping, checkpoint all progress and record the exact reason for stopping and the next executable action.
