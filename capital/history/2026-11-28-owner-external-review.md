# Owner instruction after external review, 2026-11-28 (summary of the six points; full text in the session record)

1. Procurement intelligence competition: Stotles (stotles.com/awarded-contracts) and BidSkim (bidskim.com/features/contract-expiry-tracker) already offer contract expiry intelligence. Investigate offerings, pricing, coverage and weaknesses; establish why an E4 prospect would pay £295; do not assume differentiators without evidence; continue E4 unless materially invalidated.
2. Commercial autonomy: prioritise establishing and verifying the complete cycle: discovery, customer acquisition, payment, fulfilment, support, accounting, profit measurement, reinvestment. Say which stages are operational, which are built but untested, and which need a human. The objective is an AI-operated business, not continual research.
3. Parallel opportunities: E4 primary; E3 fallback; automated business improvement services as a candidate for small targeted validation; paper trading and simulations; asset acquisition monitoring; continuous discovery. Not exclusive; identify stronger opportunities where evidence supports them.
4. Permanent management dashboard: one canonical, readable dashboard updated after every cycle, covering money, opportunities, assets, decisions, pipeline, simulations, new opportunities, capabilities and blockers, owner actions, next actions. Distinguish verified results, estimates, assumptions and untested capabilities. Keep it concise; not a software project.
5. Reduce owner involvement: identify what legally or technically needs the owner and consolidate it; once operational, propose narrowly scoped standing authorisations with limits, monitoring, audit trails and emergency stops. Target under 30 minutes a week.
6. Prioritise execution: obtain genuine commercial evidence through controlled experiments; do not restart research or rewrite the mandate; stay within existing boundaries.
