## Rules
- Limited companies and LLPs only, confirmed active on Companies House. No sole traders or partnerships.
- Generic addresses (info@, sales@, enquiries@) preferred. Named addresses only with the privacy notice linked.
- One email per company, plus at most one follow-up after 7 days. No more than 20 a day.
- Every factual line must be true for that recipient and traceable to the prospects file (contract, buyer, website, plan status).
- No claim of affiliation with the NHS or government. No "legal requirement" wording: it is a procurement condition.
- Opt-outs go on a suppression list the same day.
- Framework suppliers: say plainly that tenders starting after 1 April 2027 will ask frameworks for a wider plan than the standard one, until the wider version is built.
