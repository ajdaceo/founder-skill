# Client data checklist (sent after a "yes")
For the baseline year and the latest full financial year:
1. Electricity: kWh per site (from bills)
2. Gas / other heating fuel: kWh or litres
3. Company vehicles: litres of diesel/petrol, or miles by vehicle type
4. Business travel: miles by car (grey fleet), rail, air
5. Employee commuting: headcount, rough average one-way distance and mode (estimate is acceptable, stated as such)
6. Waste: tonnes or skip collections by type, and disposal route if known
7. Upstream/downstream transport by third parties (if known)
8. Completed and planned carbon reduction actions (LED, EVs, renewable tariff...)
9. Net zero target year (default 2050) and director name/role for sign-off
