## Carbon Reduction Plans for NHS suppliers. Fixed price £249.

From 1 April 2027, new NHS tenders above the procurement threshold ask for a full published Carbon Reduction Plan as a pass or fail condition, where a one-line Net Zero Commitment is accepted today for most contracts under £5 million a year. New NHS frameworks already ask for one. (NHS England, Carbon Reduction Plan and Net Zero Commitment requirements, guidance of 9 June 2026: {guidance_url}.)

Who needs one: suppliers bidding for NHS procurements as the NHS applies its rules, and central government contracts over £5 million a year under PPN 006. Other public buyers decide for themselves. Check your tender documents.

### What you get
- A Carbon Reduction Plan in the official PPN 006 format: Scope 1, Scope 2 and the five required Scope 3 categories, baseline and current year, targets, measures and the declaration block.
- Calculated with the UK Government greenhouse gas conversion factors for each reporting year, by software, not by guesswork. A calculation record shows every line.
- Word and PDF versions, plus a one-page note on signing, publishing and the NHS's free plan-checking service.
- One round of corrections within 30 days. A reminder before your annual update is due.

### How it works
1. Pay £249 (card or bank transfer).
2. Fill in one form: energy, fuel, travel, commuting estimate and waste totals. About 30 minutes with your bills to hand.
3. Receive your plan within 5 working days of complete data.
4. Your director checks it, signs it and publishes it on your website.

### What we do not do
