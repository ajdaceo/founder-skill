# Statutory request text (FOR OWNER APPROVAL; not sent)

To be sent from hello@oakmarch.co.uk in the owner's name to public bodies with no live published contract in the market. First batch: English councils and police forces (about 280 bodies), no more than 60 a day. One request per body.

Subject: Freedom of Information request: occupational health and employee assistance contracts

Dear Freedom of Information team,

Under the Freedom of Information Act 2000, please provide the following for your organisation's current arrangements for (a) occupational health services and (b) an employee assistance programme:

1. The name of the supplier, or confirmation that the service is provided in-house.
2. The contract start date and end date.
3. Any extension options remaining, and their length.
4. The annual contract value, or the most recent full year's spend.
5. Whether it was let through a framework agreement and, if so, which one.
6. The planned date for re-procurement, if one has been decided.

If one supplier provides both services under a single contract, one answer covering both is fine. Please also confirm that the information may be re-used under the Open Government Licence.

Yours faithfully,
[Owner's full name]
[Service address]

Notes for approval:
- The Act requires the applicant's real name; a trading name alone is not valid.
- The request asks for facts a contracts team holds readily. It is one short request per body, sent once.
- Replies are logged in the private repository with the date received.
