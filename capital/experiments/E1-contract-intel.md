# E1: Public-sector software renewal snapshot

Status: candidate only, not selected (2026-11-27). Evidence inherited from earlier research is unverified.

- Hypothesis: small UK software vendors selling one category to councils or NHS bodies will pay £400 for a verified table of which bodies use which supplier, at what value, ending when.
- Evidence so far: incumbents charge for this (Tussell ~£11,400/licence/yr; Stotles £50–£475/month); 9,416 published IT contracts end in the 12 months to Sept 2027 (round 2 research). Raw notice feeds are commoditised (several Apify actors at ~$3 per 1,000 notices), so the offer must rest on FOI-derived incumbent and renewal data that notices don't contain.
- Customer: SMEs (limited companies) selling the chosen category into the public sector.
- Offer: "Renewal snapshot": 60 bodies, incumbent supplier, contract value, end date, source link per row; one-off £400 (no VAT: owner below threshold). Product exists before any offer is sent. Free league table and 5-row sample public.
- Channel: 40 researched emails to named vendor contacts (limited companies only, opt-out, AI disclosed); league table page as landing.
- Category criteria: at least 40 identifiable vendor companies; at least 60 bodies with a published FOI answer or award notice; not already profiled by Future Health Intelligence.
- Maximum cost: domain (≤£12) from the shared setup envelope. No ads.
- Exposure target: 40 delivered emails, sent by 2026-12-10 (before the Christmas slowdown).
- Deadline: results judged 2026-12-24.
- Pass: at least 1 independent paid order.
- Continue-with-change: 0 orders but 3+ substantive replies (price, sample, scope): one revision of price or format, then stop.
- Fail: 0 orders and fewer than 3 substantive replies. Stop E1.
- Failure classification to record: not reached (bounces/no opens) / offer / product / economics.
- Open checks before launch (Gate E): reuse terms for FOI response content (facts extracted with citation; no reproduction of documents); Contracts Finder data is OGL v3 (attribution required).
- Results: none yet.
- Next decision: none yet.
