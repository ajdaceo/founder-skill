# E6: may this list lawfully be sold? (checked 2026-11-28)

This is the operator's reading of the published terms, not a solicitor's opinion. **[V]** read at source today · **[P]** partly checked.

| What the list contains | Where it came from | Basis for selling it | Personal data? |
|---|---|---|---|
| NHS buyer, contract title, award date, notice link | Find a Tender award notices | Open Government Licence v3.0, stated in the site footer **[V]**. The licence allows commercial re-use with an attribution statement, which the file carries | No |
| Company name, number, type | Companies House public register | Public register information on a gov.uk service; facts about companies **[P]** (the register's own re-use statement was not re-read today) | No |
| Website address | The award notice or the company's own site | A fact | No |
| "No plan found" or "plan dated X", with the link | Our own observation of the company's public website on a stated date | Our own finding. No text or document is copied; the link points to the company's page | No |
| Published company email (183 rows) | The company's own website | A published business contact address of a company. Addresses that look like a named person are left out and the builder refuses to release a file containing one | No, on the ICO's reading that a generic company address is not personal data |

**Excluded on purpose:** named individuals and their addresses; sole traders and partnerships; anything from behind a login; any address copied from an award notice.

**Rules that apply to the buyer, and how they are told:** unsolicited email may go only to companies and LLPs, with sender identity and an opt-out. Stated in the terms, the delivery email and the file's "Read me" sheet.

**Accuracy of what is said about each company:** every statement is limited to what the website showed on the check date, carries its evidence link, and the error rate is disclosed. A full re-check is required within seven days before the first sale (`ops/E6-RECHECK.md`).

**Non-exclusivity:** stated in the first email, the terms, the "Read me" sheet, the delivery email and the template reply.

**Payment provider:** Stripe's restricted-businesses list (last updated 22 September 2026) **[V]** does not mention lead lists, marketing lists or data sales. It prohibits telemarketing, "sales of online traffic or engagement", anything infringing "privacy rights of any third party" and anything unlawful where the business is located. A list of companies with no personal data falls under none of these on the text. Stripe keeps discretion and says the list "is not exhaustive".

**Mailbox provider:** Microsoft's terms bar bulk spam, not individually written business email **[V]** (set-up guide).

**Residual risks, stated plainly**
- A company's website terms may forbid collecting its address for marketing. The addresses are published business contacts and are a small part of the product; if you prefer the most cautious version, say "E6 without emails" and the column is dropped.
- If a "generic" address in fact identifies one person, it is personal data. The filter reduces this; it cannot remove it entirely.
- The ICO fee exemption (set-up step 7) assumes no personal data is held for sale. This list is built to keep that true.

**Conclusion:** lawful to sell on this reading, with the attribution statement, the exclusions above and the pre-sale re-check.
