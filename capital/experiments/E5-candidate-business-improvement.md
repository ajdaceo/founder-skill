# E5 (candidate): fix a measured weakness for a hand-picked set of small companies

Status: CANDIDATE kept at the owner's request (2026-11-28). Not designed in detail, not approved.

- **Idea:** software finds companies with a specific, checkable weakness, shows each the evidence, and sells a fixed-price fix delivered automatically.
- **Evidence so far:** thin (`research/mechanisms/outreach.txt`). Generic versions get 1 to 4% replies and are crowded; tools already sell the generic pitch.
- **Shape worth testing:** tens of prospects, not thousands; a weakness that costs them money in a way they can verify; UK limited companies only (lawful to email); a fix we can deliver without a conversation.
- **Head start we hold:** 564 supplier websites already checked for E3 (down, blocked or broken sites, missing statements, contact details).
- **First step when E4 is running:** pick one weakness from that data with at least 30 clear cases and a fix under £300, and write the experiment record.
