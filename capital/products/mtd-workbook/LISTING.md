# Store listing: QuarterReady MTD Workbook

**Title:** MTD Spreadsheet for Sole Traders and Landlords: Making Tax Digital Record-Keeping Workbook (Excel, Google Sheets)

**Price:** £14

**Short description:** Keep your Making Tax Digital records in one spreadsheet. Every transaction gets a date, amount and HMRC category, and the workbook totals them into cumulative quarterly figures ready for bridging software.

**Description:**
From April 2027, sole traders and landlords with more than £30,000 of qualifying income have to keep digital records and send HMRC an update every quarter. You are allowed to keep those records in a spreadsheet. This is that spreadsheet, already built.

What is inside:
- Self-employment income and expense sheets with HMRC's categories as dropdowns
- UK property income and expense sheets, with residential mortgage interest kept separate
- Cumulative quarterly summaries (the year-to-date figures each update needs) plus single-quarter figures
- Deadline sheet that calculates your update periods and due dates for any tax year, standard or calendar quarters
- Private-use column so only the allowable part of a cost is counted
- Mileage log with the flat-rate calculation
- Rough tax set-aside estimate with editable rates
- Warnings for rows missing a date or category

One .xlsx file. Works in Excel, Google Sheets and LibreOffice. No macros. No subscription. Reuse it every year by changing one cell.

Please note: this is a record-keeping template. It does not submit to HMRC. To submit from a spreadsheet you also need HMRC-compatible bridging software (several are free; see GOV.UK's list). This is not tax advice and is not affiliated with or endorsed by HMRC.
