# Market discovery, 2026-11-27

Method: web search only. Figures below are from the cited sources, not estimates of mine unless marked.

## Constraint that shapes everything
£0 budget and no audience. So the first product needs either a marketplace that already has buyers, or a dated event that makes people search. Most indie products never earn; one write-up puts the median Gumroad creator at about $72/month (dev.to/insightraider, vendor analysis, treat as rough).

## Chosen: UK Making Tax Digital for Income Tax (MTD)
Evidence
- HMRC: 864,000 sole traders and landlords were brought in from April 2026 (income over £50,000). Over £30,000 joins 6 April 2027; over £20,000 from April 2028. (gov.uk news and policy papers)
- The April 2027 group is identified from 2025-26 returns due 31 January 2027, so awareness peaks December to April. We are inside that window.
- Spreadsheets are permitted for records if linked to bridging software. (gov.uk guidance)
- People already pay for this: Etsy and Payhip listings for MTD spreadsheets at £9.59 to £34.99.
- Free bridging tools exist (My Tax Digital, Affordable MTD, aligned.tax and others), so "spreadsheet + free bridge" is a real zero-subscription route. That is the pitch.
- April 2027 joiners get no penalty points for late quarterly updates in 2027-28 (ICAEW, Oct 2026 Budget coverage).
Risks
- Crowded: free full software from Sage, Clear Books and others. Our buyer is the person who wants to stay in a spreadsheet.
- Existing paid templates. Differentiation is correctness (cumulative totals, mortgage interest, private use, mileage) and trade-specific editions.
- Not yet known: sales volume of competing listings. No data found; do not assume.
- The size of the April 2027 cohort was not confirmed in this session's sources.

## Considered and parked
- MTD filing/bridging software: needs HMRC production approval and ongoing liability. Too heavy for now; crowded with free options.
- Companies House software-only accounts filing: moved to April 2028. iXBRL filing software is a regulated-feeling build. Revisit mid-2027.
- German e-invoicing (issuing mandatory from 2027 for turnover over EUR 800k, 2028 for the rest): free tools already include ZUGFeRD output. Possible validator/converter niche; needs German-language distribution.
- EU AI Act SME documentation kits: dates in flux (possible delay to Dec 2027). Template pack is feasible; demand unproven.
- US ADA web accessibility for small governments (April 2027): buyers are public bodies, slow procurement.
