5 REGULATION
- FCA primary credit brokers: defines individual as "a sole trader, or a partnership of 2 or 3 people not all of whom are bodies corporate"; introducing them to finance providers needs Full Permission credit broking https://www.fca.org.uk/firms/authorisation/consumer-credit-brokers/primary-credit-brokers
- gov.uk: no authorisation needed for credit to another business unless customer is sole trader, partnership <4 partners or unincorporated association https://www.gov.uk/offering-credit-consumers-law (via search summary). Limited-company position: loans to companies are not 'credit agreements' (FCA permissions doc). PERG 2.7 text itself NOT retrieved: UNCONFIRMED verbatim; read https://handbook.fca.org.uk/handbook/PERG/2/7.html . Note regulated if business loan <=GBP25k to sole trader/small partnership; broader for >25k exempt agreements broking.
- ICO B2B marketing https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/direct-marketing-and-privacy-and-electronic-communications/business-to-business-marketing/ : "corporate subscriber covers subscribers that are a corporate body with separate legal status"; "sole traders and other types of partnerships are classed as individual subscribers"; named employee work details "will constitute personal data even if they are acting in their business capacity"; selling lists requires "a lawful basis for selling or buying the personal data" and telling people; PECR email rule "does not apply to corporate subscribers" but treat unclear as individual; sole traders need consent/soft opt-in; should honour opt-outs.
