COMPLIANCE
- UK PI referral fees banned since 1 Apr 2013 (LASPO s56-60); per-lead payments are a warning sign. https://www.sra.org.uk/solicitors/guidance/ban-personal-injury-referral-fees . Legal Futures: "pay per enquiry" only compliant if no client data transferred https://www.legalfutures.co.uk/latest-news/f4l-unveils-referral-fee-compliant-pay-per-enquiry-model
- FCA claims-management: lead gen (seeking out/referring claims, selling data) is regulated; market study launched 19 May 2026, interim findings expected Dec 2026. https://www.fca.org.uk/news/statements/fca-review-claims-management-practices ; https://www.freshfields.com/en/our-thinking/blogs/risk-and-compliance/the-fca-launches-market-study-into-claims-management-services-102mvdq . Consumer credit/mortgage/insurance introducing: not verified in this run (UNCONFIRMED; general knowledge says introducing/effecting introductions is regulated, check FCA perimeter).
- US TCPA one-to-one consent rule vacated by 11th Cir. 24 Jan 2025 (Insurance Marketing Coalition v FCC): https://www.mofo.com/resources/insights/250130-eleventh-circuit-vacates-fcc-s-tcpa-one-to-one-consent-rule . Later 2025-26 status: UNCONFIRMED; express written consent still required for marketing calls/texts.
