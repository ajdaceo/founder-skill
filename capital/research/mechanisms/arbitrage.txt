2 BOOKS / FLIPPING
- Amazon textbook trade-in ended 2020 (https://gauravtiwari.org/best-places-to-sell-textbooks-online). Buyback sites pay ~10-35% of retail; eBay net 40-55% of new price (US blog). World of Books min 10 items or £5 (https://sell.worldofbooks.com/en-gb/sell-textbooks). No UK per-book margin data: UNCONFIRMED.
- eBay UK: listing then buying from another retailer that ships direct to buyer is not allowed; dropship only with wholesale supplier or owned stock: https://www.ebay.co.uk/help/selling/posting-items/setting-postage-options/drop-shipping?id=4176 (snippet; exact wording not opened).
- Amazon dropshipping allowed only if you are seller of record, you handle returns, no third-party packing slips (secondary: https://casrai.org/guides/amazon-dropshipping-policy ; Amazon blog https://sell.amazon.com/blog/dropshipping-on-amazon). Wording UNCONFIRMED at source.

Example: 45% total return ~8%/yr (domaindetails). Sedo/DAN fees: UNCONFIRMED. No audited small-portfolio returns.
