REGULATION
- UK: FSMA RAO 2001 art 70 (sale of body corporate) https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2001/544/article/70 ; FCA PERG 2.9 https://handbook.fca.org.uk/handbook/PERG/2/9.html : 50%+ voting shares or day-to-day control, with conditions on parties. Applicability to a mere introducer and art 33 introducing exclusions not verified: UNCONFIRMED; take legal advice. Shares-only: asset sales not "investments".
- US: Exchange Act s15(b)(13) via Consolidated Appropriations Act 2023, effective 29 Mar 2023; EBITDA <$25m or revenue <$250m; no financing/custody; state registration not pre-empted; finders not covered https://www.goodwinlaw.com/en/insights/publications/2023/01/01_27-new-federal-ma-broker-exemption ; https://foleyhoag.com/news-and-insights/publications/alerts-and-updates/2026/july/the-m-a-broker-exemption/ . Transaction-based pay to unregistered finders is a risk (Baker Donelson link).
- PECR (ICO): corporate subscribers (companies, LLPs, some Scottish partnerships) can be emailed unsolicited; sole traders/other partnerships are individual subscribers needing consent/soft opt-in https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/direct-marketing-and-privacy-and-electronic-communications/business-to-business-marketing/ . Many small owners are sole traders/partners -> email limited. Postal: UK GDPR legitimate interests / Art 21 objection right; Companies House data reuse terms: UNCONFIRMED.
