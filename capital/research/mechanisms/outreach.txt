3 LAW
- UK PECR [PRIMARY ICO https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/direct-marketing-and-privacy-and-electronic-communications/business-to-business-marketing/]: "The PECR rule on direct marketing by electronic mail does not apply to corporate subscribers." Sole traders and some partnerships: "you can only market them by electronic mail if they have specifically consented, or the 'soft opt-in' applies." Must "not disguise or conceal your identity" and "give a valid address for business to opt-out". Live calls: "cannot call numbers registered with the Corporate Telephone Preference Service (CTPS)" nor TPS; screen both. (UK GDPR still applies to named individuals' data - UNCONFIRMED detail, not fetched.)
- US CAN-SPAM [PRIMARY https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business]: "The law makes no exception for business-to-business email." Accurate headers, non-deceptive subject, disclose it is an ad, valid physical postal address, clear opt-out honoured within 10 business days, up to $53,088 per email.
- US TCPA [SEARCH]: FCC declaratory ruling 8 Feb 2024: AI-generated voices are "artificial" under TCPA, need prior consent (https://www.fcc.gov/document/fcc-makes-ai-generated-voices-robocalls-illegal). Business texts: consent rules not confirmed. UNCONFIRMED.
- Canada CASL [SEARCH; crtc.gc.ca 403]: express consent, or implied via conspicuous publication of address only if no "do not contact" notice and message is relevant to recipient's role (CRTC guide https://crtc.gc.ca/eng/com500/guide.htm; FCA CompuFinder 2020). Burden on sender; no harvesting from third-party directories. Penalties and content rules UNCONFIRMED here (identity + unsubscribe required, from memory).
- Australia Spam Act [SEARCH https://www.acma.gov.au/avoid-sending-spam]: consent needed (express or inferred), burden on sender; unsubscribe honoured within 5 working days; inferred consent from published address: Sched 2 s4 - exact conditions UNCONFIRMED (not opened).
- EU [SEARCH, vendor blogs only, no primary]: Germany UWG s7 requires prior consent even B2B; France permissive for B2B if relevant to role, with opt-out; Netherlands, Ireland described as permissive; sole traders treated as individuals (consent) - UNCONFIRMED, need primary sources/local counsel.
- Practical lawful-without-consent set: UK limited companies/LLPs; US (CAN-SPAM compliant); France/NL/Ireland (corporate, role-relevant) UNCONFIRMED; Canada and Australia only narrowly.

4 PLATFORMS
- Google bulk sender rules [PRIMARY https://support.google.com/a/answer/81126]: >5,000 msgs/day to Gmail = bulk; spam rate must stay below 0.30%, guideline below 0.10%; all senders SPF or DKIM, bulk SPF+DKIM+DMARC; marketing messages must support one-click unsubscribe; bulk also visible unsubscribe link. A few thousand/month is far below the 5,000/day threshold but authentication and low complaints still advisable.
- Microsoft Outlook.com [PRIMARY https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/blog/microsoftdefenderforoffice365blog/strengthening-email-ecosystem-outlooks-new-requirements-for-high%E2%80%90volume-senders/4399730]: >5,000/day; SPF, DKIM, DMARC p=none minimum; from 5 May 2025 non-compliant to Junk, rejection later (550 5.7.515); clear unsubscribe.
- Google Workspace policy [SEARCH https://support.google.com/a/answer/178266]: prohibits unsolicited commercial email in volume to people with no relationship; can suspend user or whole domain. So cold email from Workspace is policy risk even if legal.
- Instantly [SEARCH]: Growth $47/mo, Hypergrowth $97-107/mo; leads credits separate (https://instantly.ai/pricing). Smartlead [SEARCH]: Base $39, Pro $94 (https://www.smartlead.ai/pricing.md). Workspace Business Starter UK GBP5.90 annual/GBP7 monthly per user ex VAT (https://workspace.google.com/intl/en_uk/pricing?region=GB). lemlist, Apollo ToS on cold email: UNCONFIRMED.
- Blocking causes: complaints over ~0.3%, no auth, unverified/bounced lists, bad domains. Specific rates UNCONFIRMED (general knowledge).
- Compliant set-up for ~3,000/mo: 2-3 secondary domains, 2 mailboxes each (6 mailboxes ~GBP35-42/mo), outreach tool ~GBP40-80/mo, domains ~GBP10-15 each/yr, verification ~GBP20-50, SPF/DKIM/DMARC, one-click unsubscribe, <50 emails/mailbox/day. Total ~GBP100-170/mo. [my estimate]
