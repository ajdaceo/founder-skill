6. UK LEGAL/TAX (partial)
- Website/domain purchase generally capital expenditure; website generating income usually "enduring asset" (ATT summary) https://www.att.org.uk/technical/news/tax-treatment-software-and-website-costs . Profits from operating = trading income (individual, sole trader; £1,000 trading allowance - general knowledge, UNCONFIRMED here). Purchase cost of intangibles not deductible against income for sole trader normally; CGT on later sale (annual exempt £3,000 per search note). VAT TOGC: gov.uk page not retrieved (404) - UNCONFIRMED; overseas seller likely no VAT charged; reverse-charge on B2B services possible. Sellers mostly non-UK so USD/FX and no UK contract law default; use written asset purchase agreement with warranties, escrow, domain via registrar push, repo transfer. Get accountant.

7. ESTIMATES (my own, NOT sourced data)
Assumptions: asking 2-3x annual claimed profit (EF small deals ~22x monthly; Flippa 1.7-2.3x). Apply haircut: ~30-50% of claimed profit unverifiable/overstated at sub-$5k unvetted tier; further 15-40% annual decline for content/affiliate; running costs (hosting, AI tools ~£30-100/month) can eat profits on <£100/month assets.
- Content/affiliate: paid 2-2.5x -> nominal yield 40-50%; expected after decline ~0-20%; worst -100%; payback 3-6 yrs or never.
- Small SaaS/micro-tool (<$5k): revenue often <$1k/yr; ask 3-10x revenue; expected return negative to ~0% unless MRR verified >£200; worst -100%. 
- Extension: UNCONFIRMED; likely similar, plus policy risk.
- Newsletter: 2-4x profit, small lists, 0-20% expected; owner-dependent.
- YouTube: trend growing but ban/monetisation risk; transfer rules ambiguous; expected 0-25%, worst -100%.
- KDP/Etsy/Stripe-dependent: account not transferable -> do not buy.
- Ecommerce store: 1.55-2.9x; inventory/ops heavy.
£1,000: one asset; expected value roughly -£300 to +£150 first year; £5,000: split 3-4 assets after £150-400 verification; expected return 0-15%/yr, wide dispersion, ~25-40% chance of net loss (my guess).
