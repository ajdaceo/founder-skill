=== 1. APIFY STORE ===
Payer: Apify bills users, pays developers monthly.
Fees: PPE: "You receive 80% of this revenue" (https://docs.apify.com/platform/actors/publishing/monetize/pricing-and-costs). Apify keeps 20% commission; "Your payout is your Actor's revenue minus Apify's commission and platform usage costs" (https://docs.apify.com/platform/actors/publishing/monetize). Platform-usage costs are deducted from PPE payouts. "Apify will never charge you if your Actor doesn't earn anything."
Models in 2026: rental retired (search summary: no new rental Actors from 1 Apr 2026, retired 1 Oct 2026 - UNCONFIRMED on official page). Pay-per-event is the main model (store page shows almost all top 100 as PPE).
Payout mechanics (https://docs.apify.com/platform/actors/publishing/monetize/monthly-payouts): min "$20 for PayPal and Wise", "$100 for other payout methods", below that rolls over. Invoice generated 11th, auto-approved 14th, paid days 21-25. KYC mandatory ("complete the Know Your Customer (KYC) verification process"); individual needs legal ID. Intermediary bank fees "$10 to $50". Currency: dollars; explicit payout currency not stated. Tax: not mentioned.
Scale: press release 3 Nov 2025 (https://natlawreview.com/press-releases/apify-bets-1m-independent-developers-building-ais-missing-tools): 8,000+ Actors (that count looks like a public-Actor figure), 46,000 active developers, 25,000 customers, "more than $4 million" paid to developers since launch, $563k+ paid in Sept 2025 (6x prior year), $27M ARR. Apify $1M Challenge 3 Nov 2025-31 Jan 2026: $2 per monthly active user per Actor (min $100 at 50 users, max $2,000 at 1,000 users).
Store page (https://apify.com/store?sortBy=popularity&pricingModel=PAY_PER_EVENT, fetched 28 Nov 2026) says 137,000+ Actors (conflicts with 8,000 figure; likely includes private/all - UNCONFIRMED which). Paid count not shown.
Top by users: Google Maps Scraper 716K, Instagram Scraper 506K, TikTok 408K, Instagram Profile 308K, Google Search 244K, RAG Web Browser 215K, Instagram Reel 209K, LinkedIn Jobs (curious_coder) 185K, Website Content Crawler 182K. Many near-duplicates in each category (e.g. 4+ Facebook search scrapers, 5+ LinkedIn "no cookies", 3 Reddit, 2 Amazon reviews 19K vs 8.6K). Store page shows no ratings in the fetched view.
Earnings distribution: no official median or share-earning-nothing found. Apify claim (https://blog.apify.com/make-regular-passive-income-developing-web-automation-Actors-b0392278d085/ via search summary): top independent creator "over $4,000 a month", "several others who make more than $1,000 every month" (dated claim, unverified). Typical paid price ~$100/month in old rental days. Avg: $4M+ over several years across tens of thousands of devs implies tiny mean. Sept 2025 $563k / 46,000 devs = ~$12 mean/dev/month (my arithmetic; most devs unpaid; payout concentrated).
Newcomer evidence: Apify blog by developer of 98 Actors (https://blog.apify.com/building-98-actors-on-apify-store/): after 6 months 2,500 total users, 855 monthly active users across 98 Actors; "Months 1-2: Single-digit weekly runs. Months 3-4: Double-digit user counts... Revenue became meaningful but inconsistent."; refuses to post revenue; 15-20 h/week; "The Store has 5 to 15 competing Actors per major site," several by Apify itself; wins came from underserved regional/vertical niches (job boards, B2B directories, real estate, Welcome to the Jungle, Skool, Storeleads). Per-Actor users in niche Actors: 2-60.
Demand data available: monthly users, total runs, rating, last-modified, pricing visible on listing; third-party store scrapers exist (e.g. apify.com/khadinakbar/apify-store-scraper, apify.com/jancurn/public-actors-fetcher) so full-store analysis of users vs rating vs update date is doable at near zero cost. Apify store docs: monthly users = unique users in last 30 days (https://docs.apify.com/academy/build-and-publish/apify-store/how-store-works). Store terms (updated 15 Sep 2026): Faulty Actor for 30+ days may be deprecated/removed (https://docs.apify.com/legal/store-publishing-terms-and-conditions).
Poorly-served high-user categories: I could NOT pull ratings/last-update (UNCONFIRMED). Observable: Facebook Search Scraper (legacy, 1.7K users) page tells users to migrate - evidence of churn in Facebook scrapers; Instagram/Facebook/LinkedIn have dozens of competing Actors with 100k+ users concentrated in few.
Legal: Apify blog (https://blog.apify.com/is-web-scraping-legal/) - scraping publicly available data generally legal; login-only, personal data, IP, confidential data need care. Meta v Bright Data (N.D. Cal. Jan 2024): contract claim failed for logged-off scraping (https://techcrunch.com/2024/01/24/court-rules-in-favor-of-a-web-scraper-bright-data-which-meta-had-used-and-then-sued). Meta settled/pursued other scrapers (BrandTotal, Octopus). Not legal advice; UK GDPR for personal data is a further risk (UNCONFIRMED here, my inference). The publisher is the developer, so exposure sits with the developer.
Main risk: saturation + maintenance burden of scrapers + Apify's own first-party Actors undercut; income capped by user count.

=== 2. AGENT-AS-BUYER MARKETS ===
x402: raw counts huge but commercial volume tiny. Search summary: Dec 2025 ~731k tx/day; Feb 2026 ~57k/day (https://beincrypto.com/x402-transactions-drop-in-feb); Visa/Artemis adjusted volume far smaller (https://thedefiant.io/converge/infrastructure/visa-s-sheffield-pegs-adjusted-x402-volume-at-19m); top ~4,000 wallets ~90% of adjusted volume; TRM Labs "nothing in the protocol requires an agent" (https://www.trmlabs.com/trm-tech-blog/whos-actually-paying-measuring-ai-agent-payments-onchain); Glenbrook "200 million transactions later the real volume is still tiny" (https://glenbrook.com/payments_news/the-x402-foundation-activated-a-27-year-old-internet-code-200-million-transactions-later-the-real-volume-is-still-tiny/); CoinDesk via Forkast: real daily commercial ~ $28,000 (March 2026) (UNCONFIRMED, second-hand). x402 Foundation under Linux Foundation 14 Jul 2026. Settlement in USDC; UK individual needs crypto wallet (no KYC by protocol; off-ramp KYC/tax by HMRC). Stripe/Tempo MPP: ~$15.0M adjusted volume, 109.6M tx since May 2025 (Visa/Artemis, as of 21 Apr 2026, via search summary).
Visa Intelligent Commerce / Mastercard Agent Pay / Stripe ACP / Google UCP: these serve merchants selling goods, no volume published; Mastercard "first agentic transaction" Q3 2025. Not a supplier market for a software individual (needs to be a merchant). UNCONFIRMED any payouts.
MCP monetisation: 15,930+ servers (PulseMCP/Smithery/official registry, May 2026) (https://dev.to/t49qnsx7qtkpanks/15000-mcp-servers-97m-monthly-downloads-and-almost-no-one-getting-paid-4g7b); "95% of MCP server developers earn nothing" (unsourced, author sells competing product). Other post (https://dev.to/nicolas_fainstein/the-state-of-mcp-monetization-in-2026-28i4): <5% monetise, <0.5% over $1,000/mo; medians among monetisers: financial data $2,000, security $1,500, database $800, API integration $400, dev utilities $200, content $100 - self-described "directional estimates", authors have commercial interest. Agent Bazaar 18% fee; MCPize 85% share; MCP Marketplace 85/15 via Stripe Connect (all via search summaries, vendor claims). No audited marketplace payout totals found. Apify itself is the only MCP-related venue with audited-ish payout totals (Apify Actors are exposed as MCP tools; https://apify.com/mcp/developers).
AWS Marketplace "AI agents and tools": real seller programme (https://docs.aws.amazon.com/marketplace/latest/userguide/getting-started-ai-agents.html); needs AWS seller registration (business/tax/bank KYC), container MCP servers must use stateless streamable-http; standard listing fees apply (rate not retrieved: UNCONFIRMED); no published seller earnings. Buyer base is enterprise; poor fit for zero-capital individual (UK individual sole trader possible - UNCONFIRMED).

=== 3. OTHER SUPPLIER MARKETS (one-liners) ===
Shopify App Store: fee 0% on first $1M gross per developer since 1 Jan 2025, 15% after (https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/launch/distribution/revenue-share). Only distribution data found is a 2021 third-party study: 54.5% of 2,265 developers under $1k; new apps median $0 (https://spur-i-t.com/blog/shopify-app-store-analysis-2021/). Newcomer earning in 3 months: no evidence; apps need review and merchants discover via search/reviews.
Chrome Web Store: paid extensions/payments shut down Feb 2021 (https://developer.chrome.com/webstore/cws-payments-deprecation); must use third-party billing. Not a billing ecosystem.
Poe: price-per-message since Apr 2024, creators paid in dollars (https://creator.poe.com/docs/creator-monetization); Poe can modify/discontinue anytime (https://poe.com/pages/earnings-tos); no 2026 earnings data (UNCONFIRMED); US-first historically.
Replicate/fal/Hugging Face: no confirmed creator revenue share found; fal creator marketplace only reported by Sacra as in development (UNCONFIRMED). Not a newcomer earner.
RapidAPI, Clay, Zapier/Make, Bubble, monday.com: NOT researched within budget; UNCONFIRMED. (RapidAPI only referenced as "historical" in dev.to.)

=== 4. SHARP-GROWTH SUPPLY-SHORT MARKETS ===
Human-data / evaluation work platforms (Outlier/Scale, Mercor, Braintrust, Surge, Turing, Terac): pay hourly or per task, weekly pay; stated ranges $35-80/h prompt & benchmark creation, $60-150/h expert evaluation, Mercor $70-120/h ML, avg freelance AI trainer ~$31/h early 2026 (https://usebraintrust.com/ai-training, https://mercor.com/resources/experts/freelance-ai-training-jobs-guide/, https://cleverx.com/blog/companies-building-rl-environments-2026). All self-reported marketing; shortage claim is vendor claim (UNCONFIRMED independent). This is labour (selling hours), not a software product, and gated by screening/domain credentials and project availability. RL-environment builders (Terac $120/h job post) are a software-ish niche but employer-contract, not marketplace. No public demand-by-niche data.

=== CROSS-CUTTING ===
Only Apify publishes per-listing demand (monthly users, runs, rating, last update) that can be mined for niches. MCP directories show downloads/stars not revenue. No ecosystem publishes median supplier earnings.
