UK retail automated trading - research notes (28 Nov 2026). Many items rely on search-engine summaries, not full page reads; flagged UNCONFIRMED where so.

== 1. EVIDENCE ON OUTCOMES
- COBS 22.5 FCA Handbook: firms must show "[x]% of retail investor accounts lose money when trading CFDs with this provider". https://handbook.fca.org.uk/handbook/cobs22/cobs22s5
- ESMA 2018 intervention: "74-89% of retail accounts typically lose money", avg loss EUR1,600-29,000. https://www.esma.europa.eu/node/84933
- One broker disclosure (search snippet): 58.9% lose. Third-party aggregate 69.9% mean across 14 FCA brokers (theinvestorscentre.co.uk, not FCA). FCA 2017 dataset: 82% / 76% / 78% quoted by secondary sources. UNCONFIRMED: no FCA-wide 2025/26 figure found.
- FCA CFD rules PS19/18: leverage 30:1 to 2:1 by volatility; FCA estimates retail saves GBP267m-451m/yr. https://www.fca.org.uk/publication/policy/ps19-18.pdf
- Chague, De-Losso, Giovannetti "Day Trading for a Living?" (Brazil futures 2013-15): 97% of those persisting >300 days lost money; 1.1% earned > min wage; 0.5% > bank teller starting salary. https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=3423101
- Barber & Odean "Trading is Hazardous to Your Wealth": costs and trading frequency explain poor household returns. https://faculty.haas.berkeley.edu/odean/Papers%20current%20versions/Individual_Investor_Performance_Final.pdf
- McLean & Pontiff (J Finance 2016, 97 predictors): returns 26% lower out of sample, 58% lower post-publication. https://econpapers.repec.org/RePEc:bla:jfinan:v:71:y:2016:i:1:p:5-32
- Hurst, Ooi, Pedersen "A Century of Evidence on Trend-Following Investing" (AQR): 67 markets since 1880; net Sharpe ~0.76 after costs and 2/20 fees per secondary commentary (UNCONFIRMED vs paper). https://www.aqr.com/insights/research/journal-article/a-century-of-evidence-on-trend-following-investing
- Not found: Moskowitz-Ooi-Pedersen decay evidence; typical drawdown figures (UNCONFIRMED; trend-following drawdowns of 20-30%+ commonly reported, need citation).

== 3. TAX
- CGT 2026-27: annual exempt amount GBP3,000; 18% basic / 24% higher. https://www.gov.uk/capital-gains-tax/rates (fetched, quoted)
- Trading vs investing: HMRC manual fetch 404; secondary sources say HMRC presumes individual share/crypto activity is investment, but frequent organised trading can be treated as trade (income tax) - UNCONFIRMED from HMRC.
- Crypto disposals: CGT on sell, swap, spend, gift (secondary: IG). Confirm on gov.uk.
- Stamp duty 0.5% on UK-listed shares (SDRT); most UK-domiciled/Irish ETFs exempt; US shares not UK SDRT (secondary: IG; verify).
- Spread betting: generally free of CGT and stamp duty unless HMRC deems it trading; losses not deductible (secondary). Betting winnings not taxable (background knowledge, UNCONFIRMED here).
- ISA: Trading 212 Stocks ISA has API; IBKR ISA UNCONFIRMED.
