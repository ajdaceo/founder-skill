# Opportunity radar

Weekly, capped (see `OPERATING.md`). Score each item 0 to 3 on: money already changing hands, a cheap test exists, our edge, time pressure. Promote at 8 or more, or when a test would cost almost nothing.

## Watchlist (what to re-check each week)
- Regulation and deadlines that force businesses to buy something by a date.
- UK award notices (pipeline in `ops/`): new categories, buyers or surges.
- Marketplace demand data that is public (for example the Apify store pull in `ops/apify_store_probe.py`).
- Small assets for sale with verifiable revenue (Flippa, Microns, Acquire and similar).
- Platform rule changes that open or close a channel.
- New technical capabilities that remove a cost somebody still charges for.
