QuarterReady

Making Tax Digital records, in a spreadsheet you own

From 6 April 2027, sole traders and landlords with more than £30,000 of qualifying income must keep digital records and send HMRC an update every quarter. QuarterReady is a ready-made workbook that keeps those records and totals them into HMRC's categories.

What you get

  • Transaction sheets for self-employment and property: date, amount and category on every row, with dropdowns using the same categories HMRC asks for.
  • Cumulative quarterly summaries. Each update to HMRC covers the year to date, and the workbook calculates exactly that, plus single-quarter figures for your own use.
  • Deadline sheet that works out your four update periods and due dates for any tax year, with standard or calendar quarters.
  • Private-use column so only the allowable part of a mixed cost is counted.
  • Mileage log with the flat-rate calculation, including the drop in rate after 10,000 miles.
  • Mortgage interest handled correctly for landlords: shown separately, not deducted from rental profit.
  • Rough tax set-aside estimate with editable rates.
  • Error flags for rows missing a date or category, or dated outside the tax year.

What it does not do

QuarterReady keeps your records. It does not send anything to HMRC. To submit, you also need HMRC-compatible bridging software that reads the totals from a spreadsheet; several are free, and GOV.UK publishes the current list. QuarterReady's summary sheets have a fixed layout so they are simple to link.
