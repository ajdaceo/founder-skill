CAPITAL — INVESTMENT AND SCOPE CLARIFICATION (owner, 2026-11-28)

I want to clarify my willingness to invest and the scope of CAPITAL.

I am willing to invest money where credible evidence demonstrates an opportunity to generate a return. However, my disposable income is limited, so CAPITAL should progressively become self-financing rather than relying on continuous funding from me.

Do not interpret the existing budget as a requirement to build a business for virtually nothing. It is an initial capital allocation for discovering and validating opportunities.

I have seen examples of autonomous agents that:

* Execute trades using predefined capital allocations and risk limits.
* Identify businesses with poor Google visibility, outdated websites or other measurable commercial weaknesses and offer solutions.
* Monitor markets for pricing discrepancies and commercial opportunities.
* Acquire, improve and monetise existing assets.
* Automate customer acquisition, sales and service fulfilment.

These are illustrative examples only. Do not anchor your research around them or assume I am instructing you to pursue any particular one.

Investigate the full range of legitimate mechanisms through which autonomous agents, software, information and capital can generate profit.

For promising opportunities, establish:

* Actual evidence of profitability.
* Minimum required investment.
* Working capital requirements.
* Expected and worst-case financial outcomes.
* Time until potential revenue and capital recovery.
* Ability to automate execution.
* Scalability and reinvestment potential.
* Competitive advantage.
* Legal, regulatory and platform requirements.

Do not reject an opportunity simply because it requires investment. Determine what investment would be required and whether the evidence justifies presenting it to me.

Equally, do not assume that additional funding will be available every month.

Where financial trading is investigated, begin with historical validation, realistic transaction costs, out-of-sample testing and paper trading. Do not deploy real money, use leverage or access financial accounts without separate explicit approval.

Where automated commercial outreach is investigated, ensure lawful targeting, platform compliance, truthful offers and reliable fulfilment.

Consider whether CAPITAL can eventually manage multiple independent income-generating operations, allocating retained profits towards opportunities with the strongest demonstrated risk-adjusted returns.

Continue the existing procurement intelligence investigation where commercially worthwhile, without allowing it to dominate your wider research.

Maintain all existing budget, approval, security and legal boundaries.

Think of CAPITAL as an autonomous commercial operator and capital allocator, not merely a digital product developer.

Your objective is to generate, preserve and compound capital through measurable economic activity.
